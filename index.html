Twinspotter

A browser extension for UK shoppers. On an Amazon UK product page, it checks whether the same item is sold on AliExpress or Temu, and shows the price and delivery time side by side.

How it works

  1. On an Amazon UK product page, click Spot the twin in the extension's panel. Nothing happens until you click.
  2. The extension compares the product's photo and title with AliExpress and Temu listings.
  3. It shows any match with its price in pounds and estimated delivery, plus signs that a product may be a generic import (an unknown brand, a third-party seller).

Affiliate disclosure

Twinspotter is a member of the AliExpress and Temu affiliate programmes (Temu through Awin). Links to AliExpress and Temu shown in the extension may earn us a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. This is shown in the store listing, before install and in the extension's panel.

  • Affiliate links appear only in the extension's own panel and open only when you click them.
  • No automatic redirects, no cookies set in the background, and no changes to retailer pages, and no paid search or brand bidding.
  • No coupons, cashback or rewards.

Privacy

When you click Spot the twin, the extension sends only the product's Amazon ID, title, price, brand and image link to our server to find matches. It doesn't collect your name, email, browsing history or Amazon account details, and it doesn't run on any other site.

Availability

Twinspotter is in private testing in the UK. It will be published on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons once testing is complete.
